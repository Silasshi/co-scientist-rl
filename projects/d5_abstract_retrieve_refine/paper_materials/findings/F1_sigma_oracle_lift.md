# F1 — σ baseline (frozen + oracle inference) is the dominant lift

## Headline

Putting an Opus-extracted methodological oracle (slim, ≤4096 tokens) into the prompt at
inference time lifts a frozen Qwen3-30B-A3B from **15.62 → 25.25** on the 9-dim isolated
audit (max 45). This is **+9.63 absolute points** — the single largest effect in the
entire D5 baseline matrix, far exceeding any training-method delta.

## Numbers

Source: `runs/2026_04_26_phase2F_audit_isolated/audit_v3_isolated_summary.md`

| Baseline | Setup | Mean /45 | Std | Δ vs ξ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ξ | frozen Qwen3-30B + goal only | 15.62 | 2.87 | — |
| **σ** | **frozen Qwen3-30B + slim oracle inference** | **25.25** | 2.44 | **+9.63** |

For comparison, every training method (α, β, μ-v2) lands within ±2 of σ — training adds
much less than the inference-time oracle does.

## Per-dim breakdown

ξ → σ improvements (per-dim means):

| Dim | ξ | σ | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| U1 Soundness | 1.62 | 3.00 | +1.38 |
| U2 Significance | 2.25 | 3.50 | +1.25 |
| U3 Originality | 1.38 | 2.38 | +1.00 |
| U4 Clarity | 2.62 | 3.75 | +1.13 |
| U5 Reproducibility | 1.75 | 2.62 | +0.87 |
| T1 Necessity-of-TTT | 1.62 | 3.12 | +1.50 |
| T2 Disentanglement | 1.62 | 2.38 | +0.76 |
| T3 Compute accounting | 1.50 | 2.00 | +0.50 |
| T4 Reward-hacking awareness | 1.25 | 2.50 | +1.25 |

The lift is broad-spectrum — every dimension improves by ≥0.5 points. T1 (necessity of
test-time training framing) and U1 (claim soundness) gain the most, suggesting the oracle's
methodological scaffolding helps the model frame the problem as a TTT-discovery task and
back claims with the patterns extracted from the bibliography.

## What this means for the paper

- **Inference-time scaffolding works** — even before any training, the oracle provides
  the bulk of D5's value. This is the result that establishes σ as the production
  baseline for all subsequent training comparisons.
- **The 30B model has the capacity to use a structured oracle** — it's not a capacity
  issue, it's a content-availability issue. Without oracle the model stays at 15.62.
- **σ is the hardest baseline to beat** — δ (frozen + ref plan in context) only
  matches it (25.12), and α (Opus distillation) and β (ref SFT) fall slightly below.
  Only μ-v4 at iter 4 beats σ (28.00).

## Caveat

The oracle was generated by Opus 4.7. The "ceiling estimate" framing (ORACLE_DESIGN_v1.md)
means real-pipeline retrieval would generate a noisier oracle and σ' might be lower than
σ. Phase 3 retrieve-then-generate work will measure this gap.
